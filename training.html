03 / ALL EXPERIENCE LEVELS

Curious about a new sport?

New to
lacrosse?

No previous lacrosse experience is required. We welcome students who are curious about trying a new sport, as well as students who have played lacrosse before. Sessions will introduce the basic rules, equipment, movement patterns, and core skills step by step.

04 / ACTIVITIES

A little bit of everything

What we do

✳BEGINNER

Beginner Lacrosse Sessions

Start with the basics in a supportive setting. No experience needed—just bring your curiosity.

⌁SKILLS

Stick Skills and Passing

Build control and confidence through progressive catching, cradling, and passing drills.

◉ALL LEVELS

Small-Sided Games

Learn by playing, practise decisions, and enjoy friendly game scenarios at every level.

↗SKILLS

Shooting and Finishing

Explore accuracy, technique, and creative ways to finish around goal.

⤴FITNESS

Speed, Agility, and Fitness

Develop movement, coordination, and match-ready fitness through varied activities.

✺COMMUNITY

Team-Building Activities

Get to know one another, build trust, and shape the student community together.
