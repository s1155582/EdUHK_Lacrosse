# EdUHK Lacrosse Team

**EdUHK Lacrosse** is the official online home of The Education University of Hong Kong’s lacrosse team. It introduces the team, shares training and competition information, and welcomes students who want to try lacrosse or support the squad.

**Live website:** https://s1155582.github.io/EdUHK_Lacrosse/

## About the Team

EdUHK Lacrosse brings together students who enjoy fast-paced team sport, athletic development, and being part of a supportive sporting community. The team provides opportunities for players of different experience levels—from complete beginners to experienced athletes—to train, compete, and grow together.

Lacrosse is a dynamic sport that combines speed, coordination, stick skills, teamwork, and tactical awareness. At EdUHK, the team focuses not only on performance, but also on building confidence, discipline, sportsmanship, and lasting friendships among its members.

## What We Do

The team’s activities are centred on helping members develop as lacrosse players and as a unified squad.

- **Regular training sessions** to develop fundamental skills, fitness, game awareness, and teamwork.
- **Beginner-friendly introduction** for students who are new to lacrosse and want to learn in an encouraging environment.
- **Team preparation** for friendly matches, inter-university competitions, and other lacrosse events.
- **Team-building activities** that strengthen communication, trust, and team identity.
- **Community and campus engagement** to raise the profile of lacrosse at EdUHK.

## Joining the Team

No previous lacrosse experience is required to get involved. Students who are interested in trying the sport, improving their fitness, meeting new people, or representing EdUHK are welcome to reach out.

New members can expect:

- A welcoming introduction to the basic rules, equipment, and skills of lacrosse.
- Structured training that gradually builds stick handling, passing, catching, movement, and game understanding.
- Opportunities to train with and learn from current team members.
- A pathway from beginner sessions towards team selection and competition, where appropriate.

If you would like to join, attend a training session or contact the team through the details below.

## Training and Events

Training times, venues, and upcoming events are shared through this website and the team’s official communication channels.

Because schedules can change during examination periods, holidays, weather disruptions, or competition seasons, always check the latest updates before attending a session.

## Our Team Culture

EdUHK Lacrosse is built around more than winning matches. The team values:

- **Commitment** — attending training, preparing well, and supporting teammates.
- **Respect** — treating teammates, opponents, officials, and coaches fairly.
- **Growth** — improving individually while helping others improve.
- **Inclusion** — creating a space where beginners and experienced players can contribute.
- **Team spirit** — representing EdUHK with pride, energy, and sportsmanship.

## Support the Team

Students, alumni, staff, and supporters can help EdUHK Lacrosse by attending matches, sharing team updates, volunteering at events, or encouraging new players to join.

Businesses, sports organisations, and EdUHK departments interested in supporting the team through sponsorship, equipment, facilities, or event collaboration are also welcome to make contact.
