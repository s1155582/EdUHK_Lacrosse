02 / THE GROUP

Our story so far

Starting from
a shared interest

We are a group of passionate students at The Education University of Hong Kong who are interested in lacrosse, physical activity, teamwork, and student-led sport.

Some of us have previous playing experience, while others are discovering lacrosse for the first time. We are building a welcoming community where students can learn the sport, develop their skills, stay active, and connect with others.

Our longer-term aim is to establish an EdUHK lacrosse sports team during the 2026–27 academic year.

03 / OUR GOAL

The journey to 2026–27

Help us
build the team.

We are currently building the foundation of the group. Every committed player brings us closer to forming an EdUHK lacrosse sports team in the 2026–27 academic year.

Reaching 18 committed players gives us the squad depth that regular training, inter-university fixtures, and official recognition all depend on. It is the number that turns an interest group into a team.
