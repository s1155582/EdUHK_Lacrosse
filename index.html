01 / ABOUT

A new student community

Who we are

We are a group of passionate students at The Education University of Hong Kong who are interested in lacrosse, physical activity, teamwork, and student-led sport.

Some of us have previous playing experience, while others are discovering lacrosse for the first time. We are building a welcoming community where students can learn the sport, develop their skills, stay active, and connect with others.

Our longer-term aim is to establish an EdUHK lacrosse sports team during the 2026–27 academic year.

04 / ACTIVITIES

A little bit of everything

What we do

✳BEGINNER

Beginner Lacrosse Sessions

Start with the basics in a supportive setting. No experience needed—just bring your curiosity.

⌁SKILLS

Stick Skills and Passing

Build control and confidence through progressive catching, cradling, and passing drills.

◉ALL LEVELS

Small-Sided Games

Learn by playing, practise decisions, and enjoy friendly game scenarios at every level.

↗SKILLS

Shooting and Finishing

Explore accuracy, technique, and creative ways to finish around goal.

Develop movement, coordination, and match-ready fitness through varied activities.

Team-Building Activities
